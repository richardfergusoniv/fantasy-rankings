### League-adjusted trade grading
**Used by**: Trade Values → League-adjusted
**Market anchor**: The same unmodified FantasyCalc row and value shown in Market mode. Team need, lineup fit, cuts, waiver replacements, and dynasty posture never alter this price. The nearest available FantasyCalc format remains the league's dynasty/redraft, QB count, team count, and PPR preset; unsupported custom scoring is recorded in the dashboard and shown in the result card.
**Projection layer**: The stored weekly Vegas-consensus payload supplies projected football statistics. QB/RB/WR/TE rows are re-scored inside `getDashboard` through each league's exact Sleeper `scoring_settings`; FantasyCalc value is never substituted for projected production. The projection timestamp and any players without projections are surfaced as confidence notes.
**Roster utility**: All Sleeper rosters, their legal starter slots, and the current unrostered projection pool are returned for deterministic client grading. The same dynamic-programming legal-slot assignment used by Matchup and weekly Power Rankings handles FLEX, REC_FLEX, WRRB_FLEX, and SUPER_FLEX. Each side is optimized before and after the exact package; uneven packages cut the lowest marginal-utility player or add the best projected unrostered player until the roster returns to its original size.
**Need and surplus**: Need is explained through the lineup gain from adding the median projected available player at each QB/RB/WR/TE position. Surplus is explained through each outgoing player's removal cost after a best-available waiver refill. Weekly points drive both calculations; bye-week and contender/rebuilder inference are excluded, and dynasty uses a neutral posture.
**Draft-pick ownership**: For dynasty leagues, Sleeper's `traded_picks` endpoint moves future picks from their original roster to the current owner. FantasyCalc's neutral/mid pick row for the matching season and round supplies the displayed market value unchanged; picks are excluded from lineup deltas. When early/mid/late pick position is unknown, the result card flags the neutral market treatment.

### nflverse weekly player statistics
**Used by**: `getDashboard` Charts view and season-long/dynasty team cards
**Current-season URL**: `https://github.com/nflverse/nflverse-data/releases/download/stats_player/stats_player_week_2026.csv`
**Prior-season URL**: `https://github.com/nflverse/nflverse-data/releases/download/stats_player/stats_player_week_2025.csv`
**Test evidence**: Direct reads on 2026-09-20 returned HTTP-successful CSVs with the same 150-column weekly schema. The 2026 file was published on 2026-09-20 and includes regular-season rows with player identity, position, team, game/week, passing, rushing, receiving, kicking, defensive, EPA, opportunity-share, and fantasy-point fields. The 2025 file supplies the immediately preceding games needed to complete a true trailing 17-game sample for active 2026 players.
**Processing**: Parse regular-season rows only. QB/RB/WR/TE/K entities are keyed by nflverse player ID and keep the latest team/name. The current-season view averages each metric over that player’s available 2026 game rows; the rolling view takes that active player’s latest 17 game rows across 2026 and 2025, so missed games are not inserted as zeroes. DST rows are deterministically aggregated from credited team defensive events within each game, then averaged over the same windows. Ratios and share fields average only reported observations; counting stats average over games. The graph derives both axes, tooltips, medians, quadrant membership, and summary rows from the same returned records. Season-long and dynasty team cards group current-season player rows by team and game. They total net passing yards (gross passing yards less sourced sack yards), rushing yards, offensive touchdowns, interceptions, fumbles lost, field goals, PATs, two-point conversions, return touchdowns, defensive touchdowns, and safeties; those credited events produce the displayed season points scored. The card also derives total yards, turnovers, offensive plays per game, and pass/run attempt percentages. Missing team rows remain unavailable, and the removed implied total is no longer surfaced on the card. If either weekly source is unavailable, the Charts view and team production fields render honest unavailable states rather than synthetic observations. nflverse is not used by the player-card Boom / Bust panel.

For Current Week QB/RB/WR/TE projections, match each stored Vegas-consensus player payload to the Sleeper player directory and score its stat-line inputs through the selected league’s settings. Resolve weekly availability from Sleeper’s player `injury_status`, roster `status`, and injury notes before selecting any numeric projection. Out, inactive, IR, PUP, NFI, and suspended players project to zero; stale Vegas or Sleeper numbers cannot override those designations. Questionable and doubtful players keep their numeric projection because they have not been ruled out. These league-scored projections drive Current Week QB/RB/WR/TE rankings, waiver ordering, pregame matchup forecasts, and optimized lineups; Sleeper’s established in-game projection remains the live-game overlay for available players and actual score remains the final-game value. Kicker and DST projections and ranks use Sleeper’s weekly stat projection scored through each league’s settings.

The `projection_accuracy` ledger stores one row per league, week, and player, preserving the Vegas projection and First Down comparison at hundredth-point precision and filling the actual Sleeper fantasy score when available. Rows are updated in place during the week and retained across weeks, allowing running mean absolute error to be computed for either source without adding a UI page.

## Long-term data behavior
- **Refresh policy**: Fetch on page open and on the user-visible Refresh control; React Query holds the current response briefly. Draft ADP and market snapshots persist for one day, while live Sleeper draft picks poll every 15 seconds only while the Draft section is open. A silent daily artifact action also refreshes the cached dashboard so FantasyCalc season-long values stay current without polling the source.
- **Growth**: No user-history rows are fabricated or retained; the artifact follows the current Sleeper season/week and current First Down snapshot.
- **Ordering**: Selected league persists locally; starters remain in lineup-slot order, bench remains roster order, and full rankings sort by derived rank for the selected scoring format.
- **Time semantics**: First Down `generated_at` and the server fetch time are returned as instants and rendered viewer-local; NFL week comes from Sleeper state.

### PFN line-matchup ranks and matchup cards
**Used by**: `getpfntables` — Matchup badges and cards, player Team context; `getMatchupBoxScore` — final scores in matchup cards.
**Source**: The existing `pfn-nfl-hq` ingest already populates the cached `offensive-line`, `offense`, and `defense` tables with one row per NFL team and separate pass/run grades. The managed sports-data channel supplies the final NFL score for the exact two-team, season, and week query only after that game reports a final/closed state.
**Processing**: Matchup-page game badges open a compact card with both teams’ PFN offense, defense, and offensive-line grades plus rank. Its four line comparisons show each team’s pass blocking against the opponent’s pass defense and each team’s run blocking against the opponent’s run defense, with a six-rank advantage highlighted. Season-long and dynasty ranking rows use the PFN offensive-line table’s supplied overall rank to tint the team pill: ranks 1–10 use the existing green strength treatment, 23–32 use the existing red weakness treatment, and the middle tier remains neutral. Tapping that pill opens the team card; if the PFN table or a team match is unavailable, the rank remains unavailable rather than inferred. For completed games, the matchup card also displays the managed sports-data channel’s returned box-score string and source link. Scheduled/live games do not show a final score; a final game whose source cannot be resolved gets an honest unavailable state. Missing teams, opponents, or PFN tables render an honest unavailable state instead of inferred grades.

### Team situational offense rates
**Used by**: `getpfntables` — season-long and dynasty team cards.
**Source**: `https://hindi3.sportskeeda.com/nfl/team-stat/third-down-percentage-leaders?season=2026&type=regular`
**Test evidence**: A source read on 2026-09-30 returned all 32 NFL teams after three games, with attempts, conversions, and percentages for third downs and red-zone opportunities in the same table.
**Processing**: Fetch server-side when the team-data cache is older than six hours, parse only complete NFL team rows, normalize full team names to the app’s canonical abbreviations, and store the returned third-down and red-zone touchdown percentages with game counts and fetch time. Require at least 30 teams before replacing the cache; on a source failure, retain the last valid snapshot or render those fields unavailable. The card links to the exact source and labels the fetch date.

### Strength of Schedule ranks
**Used by**: `getDashboard` (via `ingeststagedmatchupgrades`) — the Strength of Schedule Tools subview.
**Source**: `~/workspace/props-aggregator/data/staged_matchup_grades.json`, built by `~/workspace/props-aggregator/build_matchup_grades.mjs`.
**Test command**: `node ~/workspace/props-aggregator/build_matchup_grades.mjs` (reads Sleeper state, nflverse `games.csv`, Sleeper `/stats/nfl/regular/2026/{week}` actuals).
**Processing**: Completed weeks only (weeks before the current Sleeper week, plus the current week only once every schedule game is complete). Every qualifying player line is rescored through each league's own `scoring_settings`; per-defense weekly totals are averaged across completed weeks (byes omitted, not zeroed); ranks descend by average allowed (team abbreviation tie-break). One table per league; rank 1 = softest (most allowed), rank 32 = toughest (fewest). A rerun with no newly completed week leaves the staged file untouched. The artifact ingest (`ingeststagedmatchupgrades`) persists one `strength-of-schedule-v1:{leagueId}` source-cache entry per league, ignores equal/newer data, and invalidates the main dashboard cache after a commit.
