  Chargers | Ladd McConkey set to play Sunday vs. Rams    Fri Oct 30, 04:36 PM

Chargers WR Ladd McConkey is set to play in Sunday's Week 8 game at the Rams. McConkey had been in the concussion protocol this week but was a full participant in practice on Wednesday.

Footballguys view: McConkey looked great for about the first quarter of Week 1 before suffering a rib injury. Other injuries have piled up since, and McConkey's season has been mostly lost. The Chargers can hopefully figure things out coming out of last week's bye. Until they can show that, though, McConkey is a risky RB3.
